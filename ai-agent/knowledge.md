### Longevity (based on customer feedback — varies by body chemistry)
- **40% Elixir**: shortest reported is around 8 hours (2-3 sprays);
  many customers report 1-2 full days, especially when also sprayed on
  hair and clothes
- **20% EDP**: shortest reported is around 6-12 hours (2-3 sprays)
- Longevity varies by body type/skin chemistry — always frame these as
  typical ranges, not guarantees
- Lasts noticeably longer (+4-6 hours) when applied over unscented
  lotion/moisturized skin ("layering with a base") before spraying
