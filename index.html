About Pappi · Founded by Verge

A partner’s obsession. A group chat. A brand.

Pappi started with two things colliding: my partner Larry’s fascination with perfume—and the money he kept spending on it—and a TikTok chat with friends in Singapore who loved luxury fragrance but hated the price tag.

Read the full story

I didn’t have an answer for them at first. So I went looking for one: Sephora every other day, sniffing everything I could reach, studying mixes, watching videos, flipping through books. Eventually I enrolled in a proper perfumery course and visited manufacturing hubs and suppliers for real base ingredients, until I had over 150 oils on a shelf—ready for mixing, like alchemy in a Singapore bedroom my housemates swore smelled different every time the door opened.

Some fragrances started as personal requests—friends who wanted a luxury scent without the luxury price—so I named the bottle after them. Under a Singapore-born label called VG, that idea traveled with word of mouth into Malaysia and Singapore, and the bottles kept selling.

Retrenchment hit in May 2026. It could have ended things—instead it sent me home. As a returning OFW, I qualified for financial assistance to start a business, so I made it official. My brothers were dealing with chicken skin and wanted a natural alternative, which became Savon. And the perfume line, once VG, became Pappi the moment I landed back in the Philippines—black for the dark days it took to get here, gold for what came after.

How Pappi came to be.

From a partner’s perfume habit to a name with its own story.

discoverySingapore

  1. The sparkLarry’s stash grows, and a question follows

    My partner Larry’s fascination with perfume—and the money spent on it—sits alongside a TikTok chat with Singapore friends looking for a cheaper way into luxury scents.

  2. Months of learningSephora, YouTube, and an online perfumery course

    Sniffing at Sephora every other day, studying mixes, watching countless videos, flipping through books—then enrolling in a proper course and visiting manufacturing hubs and suppliers for base ingredients.

  3. The lab150+ oils, one Singapore bedroom

    A room that housemates said smelled different every time the door opened—each blend tested side-by-side against the real designer scent, like alchemy.

  4. Naming the bottlesFragrances named after the friends who requested them

    Arianne, Anne, Ayani, Jo, Ms. Fe, Ms. Jane, Angelina, Maejoy, Khimmy, and more—each one a personal request from someone who wanted a luxury scent without the luxury price.

going to marketApril 2025

  1. April 7, 2025First bottles sold

    The start of tracked sales—over 2,000 bottles (10–30ml) sold since this date, under the original “VG” label.

  2. ExpandingDistribution into Malaysia and Singapore

    Word spread beyond the original friend group; packaging upgraded from loose bottles to named boxes.

  3. Giving backGiveaways for Filipino community groups abroad

    Plus a personalized line built specifically for gifting.

the turnMay–August 2026

  1. May 2026Retrenchment

    The job in Singapore ends—and unexpectedly, it paves the way for what comes next.

  2. August 10, 2026Preparing to embark on a new journey

    Packing up Singapore, closing one chapter.

  3. August 11, 2026Arrival in Cebu

    Home, and the start of building something official.

rebirthCebu

  1. OFW supportFinancial assistance becomes the seed capital

    As a returning OFW, assistance to start a business made the decision easy—might as well make it official.

  2. A second line is bornSavon—natural soap, for a very personal reason

    My brothers were dealing with chicken skin and wanted a natural alternative—Savon exists because of them.

  3. The rebrandPappi Suds & Scents—black for the dark days, gold for what’s next

    A new name, new bottles, a new crest—built for premium, international, and a new chapter.
